BRAND BETA | Growth Experiment | January 2026

Brand Beta launched a trial-size product for new customers. First-time customer conversion increased by 17% and CAC improved moderately. The experiment initially increased packaging cost, so headline conversion alone overstated the value. Profitability improved only after supplier renegotiation reduced packaging cost. The recommendation was to continue the experiment with a margin guardrail and weekly monitoring of conversion, CAC, gross margin and repeat purchase.
