BRAND GAMMA | Product Strategy | February 2026

Brand Gamma has strong product reviews but needs to improve first-purchase conversion. Customers hesitate at the current price, and the team is considering discounting. No trial-size product exists yet. The strategy group recommends testing whether a smaller entry pack can reduce purchase friction without damaging perceived value. Any pilot should measure first-order conversion, CAC, packaging cost, gross margin and repeat purchase. A broad launch is not approved until the evidence is clear.
