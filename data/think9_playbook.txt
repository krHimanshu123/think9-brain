THINK9 PORTFOLIO | SOP / Playbook | December 2025

Think9 growth experiments should begin with a narrow hypothesis, a controlled audience and a 30-day review. Every recommendation must name evidence, owner, metrics and a scale gate. Smaller packs can reduce purchase friction, but packaging economics and repeat purchase must be validated. Sustainable packaging is preferred when complaint reduction offsets incremental cost. Cross-brand vendor overlap should be reviewed for volume leverage. Humans approve consequential external actions.
