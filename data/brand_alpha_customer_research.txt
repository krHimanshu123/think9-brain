BRAND ALPHA | Consumer Research | February 2026

Brand Alpha customers rate the product highly, but the current large package creates purchase resistance. Interviews repeatedly mention a desire for a lower entry price and a smaller commitment for first purchase. The product team proposed a 250 ml trial pack to reduce friction. Customers say they would try the smaller format before committing to the larger pack. The controlled pilot is approved for two cities, with conversion, CAC, repeat purchase and gross margin as success metrics.

The team also found that sustainable packaging reduced complaints, although the lighter material raised unit cost. Delivery interviews mention damaged outer packaging, so protection must be included in the pilot design.
